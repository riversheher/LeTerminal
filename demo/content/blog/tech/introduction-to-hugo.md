Hugo is the world's fastest framework for building websites. Written in Go, it generates static HTML files from Markdown content and templates.

## Why Hugo?

- **Speed**: Builds thousands of pages in seconds
- **Flexibility**: Customizable with themes and shortcodes
- **Content Management**: Organize content with sections and taxonomies
