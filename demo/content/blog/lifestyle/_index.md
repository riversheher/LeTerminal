Welcome to the lifestyle section — a quiet corner of the terminal where I reflect on the human side of technology.

{{< terminal-image src="https://picsum.photos/seed/lifestylehero/600/300" alt="Minimal workspace" caption="Less screen time, more life time" >}}

In a world that never stops buzzing, I write about the practices and philosophies that help me stay grounded. This isn't about perfectly curated morning routines — it's about what actually works when you're building things, raising a family, and trying to stay sane in the digital age.

### What You'll Find Here

- {{< colored-text color="#fe8019" >}}Digital Minimalism{{< /colored-text >}} — Intentional technology use, decluttering your digital life, and reclaiming your attention in an age of distraction. Inspired by {{< terminal-link url="https://www.calnewport.com" >}}Cal Newport's work{{< /terminal-link >}}.
- {{< colored-text color="#8ec07c" >}}Productivity{{< /colored-text >}} — Practical systems that respect your energy, not just your time. Deep work, task management, and building sustainable habits.
- {{< colored-text color="#d3869b" >}}Work-Life Balance{{< /colored-text >}} — Remote work reflections, setting boundaries, and the art of doing nothing without guilt.

These posts are personal, not prescriptive. They're the lessons I've learned the hard way, shared in the hope they might save you some trouble. I believe the best productivity advice is the kind that makes you feel more human, not more efficient.

### Reading the Lifestyle Section

Navigate into this section with `cd blog/lifestyle` or just type `lifestyle` at the root prompt. Each post is a standalone essay — start anywhere that catches your interest.
