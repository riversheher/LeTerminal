Digital minimalism is a philosophy of technology use in which you focus your online time on a small number of carefully selected activities that strongly support things you value.

## Principles

1. **Clutter is costly**: Digital clutter costs you time and attention
2. **Optimization is important**: Optimize your digital tools for your goals
3. **Intentionality is satisfying**: Being intentional about technology use feels good
